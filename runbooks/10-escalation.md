## When
Whenever a person must act: credentials, business rules, code errors, ambiguous data, repeated
failures, unknown errors, or when automatic handling is not allowed.

## What a good escalation contains
- the component, the error and how many messages are affected;
- what was already tried (native retries, resends) and why it did not help;
- the evidence for the suspected cause;
- a concrete next step for the person receiving it;
- never credential values or more personal data than needed.

## Who
Credentials and connectivity: integration operations. Business rules and data content: the
business owner of the flow. Code errors: the development team.
